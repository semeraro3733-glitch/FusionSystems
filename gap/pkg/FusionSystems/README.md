# The GAP 4 package FusionSystems

Saturated fusion systems on finite *p*-groups: construction, saturation
tests, automizers, isomorphism, and the classification of all saturated
fusion systems on a given *p*-group.

This is a GAP 4 implementation of the strategy of

* C. Parker and J. Semeraro, *Algorithms for fusion systems with
  applications to p-groups of small order*, Math. Comp. 90 (2021),
  arXiv:2003.01600 (original MAGMA code:
  <https://github.com/chris1961parker/Fusion-Systems>),

with the fast search for essential subgroups of

* P. Gautam, *Fusion systems on Sylow 3-subgroups of Fischer and Monster
  sporadic groups II*, arXiv:2607.24674, Section 3 (GAP code
  <https://github.com/pete-g00/sporadics-code>),

and a saturation test which never constructs the Parker–Semeraro "fusion
graph" (see below).

## Installation

Unpack the archive in the `pkg` directory of your GAP installation (or any
directory `<dir>/pkg`, started with `gap -l ";<dir>"`) and load with

    gap> LoadPackage( "FusionSystems" );

The package needs GAP >= 4.11 and the package `autpgrp`.  Run the tests with

    gap> Test( Filename( DirectoriesPackageLibrary( "FusionSystems", "tst" ), "classify.tst" ) );

## A quick tour

    gap> F := GroupFusionSystem( PSU( 4, 3 ), 3 );   # F_S(G), S a Sylow 3-subgroup
    <fusion system on a group of order 3^6 with 2 essential classes>
    gap> Print( F );
    Fusion system on a group S of order 3^6
      the fusion system of <permutation group of size 3265920 with 2 generators>
      |Out_F(S)| = 4
      2 classes of essential subgroups of orders [ 243, 81 ] with |Out_F(E)| = [ 48, 360 ]
    gap> S := UnderlyingGroup( F );;
    gap> IsSaturated( F ); IsOpFTrivial( F ); IsPPerfectFusionSystem( F );
    true
    true
    true
    gap> L := AllFusionSystems( S );;      # all saturated F on S with O_3(F) = 1 and O^3(F) = F
    gap> ForAny( L, G -> IsIsomorphicFusionSystems( G, F ) );
    true

A fusion system is specified by generating data: the group `S` (a pc
group), automorphisms of `S` which together with `Inn(S)` generate
`Aut_F(S)`, a list of (essential) subgroups `E_i` and lists of automorphisms
generating `Aut_F(E_i)`:

    gap> F := FusionSystem( S, autSgens, [ E1, E2 ], [ gens1, gens2 ] );

The fusion system is the one *generated* by these automorphism groups; it
need not be saturated, and `IsSaturated( F )` decides whether it is.

### Main functions

| Function | Description |
|---|---|
| `FusionSystem( S, autSgens, ess, essauts )` | the fusion system generated by the given automizers |
| `GroupFusionSystem( G, p )`, `GroupFusionSystem( G, S )` | the fusion system of a finite group on a Sylow *p*-subgroup |
| `IsSaturated( F )`, `FS_IsSaturatedWithReason( F )` | the saturation test (the second returns a reason for failure) |
| `AutF( F, P )` | the automizer Aut_F(P) of any subgroup P of S |
| `FConjugacyClass( F, P )`, `IsFConjugate( F, P, Q )`, `FIsomorphism( F, P, Q )` | F-conjugacy |
| `IsFCentric`, `IsFullyNormalized`, `IsFullyCentralized`, `IsFullyAutomized`, `IsFRadical`, `IsReceptive`, `HasSurjectivityProperty` | properties of subgroups |
| `OpF( F )`, `IsOpFTrivial( F )`, `FocalSubgroup( F )`, `IsPPerfectFusionSystem( F )` | O_p(F), the focal subgroup |
| `IsIsomorphicFusionSystems( F1, F2 )`, `IsomorphismFusionSystems( F1, F2 )` | isomorphism of fusion systems |
| `ProtoEssentialSubgroups( S )` | the candidates for essential subgroups (fast method) |
| `ProtoEssentialAutomizerCandidates( S, P )` | the candidates for Aut_F(P) |
| `BorelSubgroups( S )` | the possibilities for Aut_F(S)/Inn(S) |
| `AllFusionSystems( S[, opts] )` | all saturated fusion systems on S (by default those with O_p(F) = 1 and O^p(F) = F) |
| `HasStronglyPEmbeddedSubgroup( G, p )` | does G have a strongly *p*-embedded subgroup? |

`AllFusionSystems` accepts the options `OpTriv` (default `true`),
`pPerfect` (default `true`), `OutFSOrders` (a list of admissible orders of
`Out_F(S)`), `exhaustive` (examine all S-centric subgroups as candidates
for essential subgroups instead of Gautam's candidates) and `candidates`
(a function `(S, P)` supplying automizer candidates for the proto-essential
subgroup `P`).  Set `SetInfoLevel( InfoFusionSystems, 1 )` (or 2) to follow
the computation.

## How it works

**Essential subgroups.** By [Gautam, Theorem 3.6], every essential subgroup
of a saturated fusion system on S is S-conjugate to one of the subgroups
C_S(xA/A), where A runs through the terms of a central series of S with
factors of order *p* refining the lower central series and lying in [S,S],
and xA through the elements of order *p* of S/A.  These candidates are
filtered by the Parker–Semeraro tests (S-centric, |P/Φ(P)| ≥ |Out_S(P)|²,
C_{N_S(P)}(P/Φ(P)) ≤ P, Out_S(P) a possible Sylow subgroup of a group with
a strongly *p*-embedded subgroup, Aut_S(P) ∩ O_p(Aut(P)) = Inn(P), Aut(P)
insoluble unless Out_S(P) is cyclic or quaternion).  All tests are
Aut(S)-invariant, so the tests needing Aut(P) are applied once per
Aut(S)-orbit, and orbits which are not closed under Aut(S) are discarded.
The subgroup lattice of S is never computed.

**Automorphism groups.** Aut(P) is computed with `autpgrp`; when it is
soluble all computations with subgroups of Aut(P) take place in the pc
group `PcGroupAutPGroup`, otherwise in a permutation group.

**Fusion.** Let B = Aut_F(S) ⋉ S.  Every F-isomorphism between subgroups of
S is a composite of restrictions of elements of Aut_F(S) and of Aut_F(E^s)
for the S-conjugates E^s of the essential subgroups.  The S-classes of
subgroups are the nodes of a graph which is built lazily: at a node with
representative R and for every S-conjugate E^s of an essential subgroup
containing R we record the Schreier graph of the orbit of R under
Aut_F(E^s), with the arrows conjugated into the class representatives, and
likewise for the generators of a p'-complement of Inn(S) in Aut_F(S).  The
F-class of R is the connected component of its node, and Aut_F(R) is
generated by the conjugates of the groups Aut_S(X) at the nodes X of the
component together with the loops of the component (Reidemeister–Schreier).
Only the subgroups actually asked for are ever visited.

**Saturation without the fusion graph.** The test is the criterion of
[Parker–Semeraro, Theorem 2.3] (built on [Broto–Castellana–Grodal–Levi–
Oliver, Theorem 2.2]): F is saturated iff Inn(S) is a Sylow *p*-subgroup
of Aut_F(S), every fully normalized F-centric subgroup P is fully
automized, and every fully normalized F-centric F-radical P satisfies the
surjectivity property (for P < V ≤ N_S(P) the restriction
N_{Aut_F(V)}(P) → N_{Aut_F(P)}(Aut_V(P)) is onto).  The new observation is
that the set T of subgroups of S contained in a B-conjugate of an
essential subgroup is closed under F-conjugacy and under taking subgroups,
and for a subgroup P not in T every F-morphism with source P is the
restriction of a conjugation by an element of B.  Hence the F-class of P,
Aut_F(P) and the extension condition at P are those of the saturated
fusion system F_S(B), and the conditions hold automatically.  It therefore
suffices to run the test over the F-classes of the S-centric subgroups of
the essential subgroups; these are found from the lattices of the groups
E/Z(E) (which are tiny for abelian E, and never from the lattice of S), and
their classes and automizers come from the lazy graph above.

**Classification.** `AllFusionSystems` follows Parker–Semeraro: for each
*p'*-subgroup K of Aut(S) up to conjugacy (Aut_F(S) = Inn(S) ⋊ K), the
proto-essentials are grouped into B-classes, the candidates for Aut_F(P)
(subgroups of Aut(P) with Aut_S(P) as Sylow *p*-subgroup and Out having a
strongly *p*-embedded subgroup, one for each Aut(P)-class) are expanded to
the conjugates under N_{Aut(P)}(Aut_S(P)) whose Sylow normalizer is
compatible with Aut_{N_B(P)}(P) (with equality when N_S(P) lies in no
B-conjugate of a proto-essential), the subsets of proto-essentials and the
assignments of automizers are run through up to the action of
N_{Aut(S)}(Aut_F(S)), and the surviving systems are tested for
O^p(F) = F, O_p(F) = 1 and saturation and finally reduced up to
isomorphism.  The automizer candidates are enumerated with the cyclic
extension method and the hereditary bound |H|_p ≤ |Out_S(P)|; this is a
complete enumeration whenever GAP can handle the subgroup lattice of
Out(P).  For elementary abelian P with a huge Out(P) = GL(n,p) the package
falls back to the monomial groups of the Aut_S(P)-invariant frames
(reported through `InfoFusionSystems`), or to candidates supplied through
the option `candidates`.

**Isomorphism.** F1 ≅ F2 iff some isomorphism α: S1 → S2 carries the
generating data of F1 into F2 and vice versa (containment is tested with
the automizers of F2 computed by the lazy graph); α is searched in the
coset α₀·N_{Aut(S)}(Aut_{F2}(S)) with a backtrack over the essential
subgroups.
