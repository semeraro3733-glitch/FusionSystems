FusionSystems

Saturated fusion systems on finite p-groups

Version 1.0.0

October 2026

Chris Parker

Jason Semeraro
Email: semeraro3733@googlemail.com

Abstract

The package implements the algorithms of Parker and Semeraro ([PS21]) for saturated fusion systems on finite p-groups in GAP 4: construction of fusion systems from generating automizers and from finite groups, computation of F-conjugacy classes and automizers, a saturation test, the isomorphism test and the classification of all saturated fusion systems on a given p-group. It incorporates the fast search for essential subgroups of Gautam ([Gau26]) and replaces the fusion graph of [PS21] by a lazy, graph free computation.
