  [1XAbstract[101X
  [33X[0;0YThe  package  implements  the algorithms of Parker and Semeraro ([PS21]) for
  saturated fusion systems on finite [22Xp[122X-groups in GAP 4: construction of fusion
  systems  from  generating  automizers and from finite groups, computation of
  [22XF[122X-conjugacy  classes and automizers, a saturation test, the isomorphism test
  and  the  classification of all saturated fusion systems on a given [22Xp[122X-group.
  It  incorporates the fast search for essential subgroups of Gautam ([Gau26])
  and replaces the fusion graph of [PS21] by a lazy, graph free computation.[133X
