  [1X1.1 [33X[0;0YFusion systems[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [22XS[122X  be  a  finite  [22Xp[122X-group.  A  [13Xfusion system[113X on [22XS[122X is a category [22XF[122X whose
  objects  are the subgroups of [22XS[122X and whose morphisms [22XHom_F(P,Q)[122X are injective
  group  homomorphisms  containing all those induced by conjugation in [22XS[122X, such
  that  every morphism factors as an isomorphism followed by an inclusion. The
  fusion  system  [13Xgenerated[113X  by  subgroups  [22XA_i  ≤  Aut(E_i)[122X  ([22XE_i ≤ S[122X) is the
  smallest fusion system containing them. By the Alperin–Goldschmidt theorem a
  saturated  fusion  system  is generated by [22XAut_F(S)[122X together with the groups
  [22XAut_F(E)[122X,  [22XE[122X  running  through representatives of the [22XF[122X-conjugacy classes of
  [13Xessential[113X  subgroups  (the  fully normalized [22XF[122X-centric subgroups [22XE[122X for which
  [22XOut_F(E)[122X has a strongly [22Xp[122X-embedded subgroup).[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YIn this package a fusion system is therefore given by the data[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Y[22XS[122X, a [22Xp[122X-group given as a pc group,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Yautomorphisms of [22XS[122X which together with [22XInn(S)[122X generate [22XAut_F(S)[122X,[133X
  
  [30X    [33X[0;6Ya  list  of subgroups [22XE_1,dots,E_k[122X of [22XS[122X (the essential subgroups) and,
        for each of them, a list of automorphisms generating [22XAut_F(E_i)[122X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YThe fusion system is the one generated by these groups; it is not assumed to
  be saturated. Whether it is saturated is decided by [2XIsSaturated[102X ([14X2.3-1[114X).[133X
  
  
  [1X1.2 [33X[0;0YOverview of the algorithms[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0Y[13XEssential  subgroups.[113X  By [Gau26, Theorem 3.6] every essential subgroup of a
  saturated  fusion  system  on  [22XS[122X  is  [22XS[122X-conjugate  to  one  of the subgroups
  [22XC_S(xA/A)[122X,  where  [22XA[122X  runs  through  the terms of a central series of [22XS[122X with
  factors of order [22Xp[122X refining the lower central series and contained in [22X[S,S][122X,
  and [22XxA[122X through the elements of order [22Xp[122X of [22XS/A[122X. These candidates are filtered
  with  the  tests  of  [PS21];  all  tests are invariant under [22XAut(S)[122X and the
  expensive  ones  (which  need [22XAut(P)[122X) are applied once per [22XAut(S)[122X-orbit. The
  subgroup lattice of [22XS[122X is never computed.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0Y[13XAutomorphism  groups.[113X  Automorphism groups of [22Xp[122X-groups are computed with the
  package  [5Xautpgrp[105X.  If  [22XAut(P)[122X is soluble all computations with its subgroups
  take  place  in  the  pc  group  returned  by  [10XPcGroupAutPGroup[110X; otherwise a
  permutation representation is used.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0Y[13XFusion.[113X  Let  [22XB  =  Aut_F(S)  ⋉  S[122X and let [22XC[122X be a [22Xp'[122X-complement of [22XInn(S)[122X in
  [22XAut_F(S)[122X.  Every isomorphism in [22XF[122X is a composite of restrictions of elements
  of [22XAut_F(S)[122X and of [22XAut_F(E_i^s)[122X, [22Xs ∈ S[122X. The [22XS[122X-conjugacy classes of subgroups
  form  the  nodes  of  a  graph  which  is  built  lazily:  at  a  node  with
  representative  [22XR[122X  and  for  every  [22XS[122X-conjugate [22XE^s[122X of an essential subgroup
  containing  [22XR[122X  we  record  the  Schreier  graph  of  the  orbit  of  [22XR[122X under
  [22XAut_F(E^s)[122X,  with  the arrows conjugated into the class representatives, and
  the arrows [22XR -> c(R)[122X for the generators [22Xc[122X of [22XC[122X. By the Reidemeister–Schreier
  procedure  for  groupoids the [22XF[122X-class of [22XR[122X is the connected component of its
  node and [22XAut_F(R)[122X is generated by the conjugates of the groups [22XAut_S(X)[122X, [22XX[122X a
  node of the component, and the loops of the component.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0Y[13XSaturation.[113X  By [PS21, Theorem 2.3] (which rests on [BCG+05, Theorem 2.2]) a
  fusion  system  generated  by  [22XAut_F(S)[122X  and  the  automizers  of  [22XF[122X-centric
  subgroups  is  saturated  if  and  only  if  [22XInn(S)[122X is a Sylow [22Xp[122X-subgroup of
  [22XAut_F(S)[122X,  every  fully  normalized [22XF[122X-centric subgroup [22XP[122X is fully automized,
  and  every  fully  normalized  [22XF[122X-centric  [22XF[122X-radical  [22XP[122X  has the [13Xsurjectivity
  property[113X:  for  all  [22XP  <  V  ≤  N_S(P)[122X the restriction map [22XN_Aut_F(V)(P) ->
  N_Aut_F(P)(Aut_V(P))[122X  is  surjective.  Let  [22XT[122X  be  the set of subgroups of [22XS[122X
  contained  in  a  [22XB[122X-conjugate  of  an  essential subgroup. [22XT[122X is closed under
  [22XF[122X-conjugacy  and under taking subgroups, and for [22XP ∉ T[122X every [22XF[122X-morphism with
  source  [22XP[122X  is  the  restriction  of  a  conjugation  by an element of [22XB[122X (the
  automizers  of the essential subgroups never apply). Hence the [22XF[122X-class of [22XP[122X,
  [22XAut_F(P)[122X  and the extension condition at [22XP[122X are those of the saturated fusion
  system  [22XF_S(B)[122X,  and  the  conditions hold. It therefore suffices to run the
  test  over  the  [22XF[122X-classes  of  the  [22XS[122X-centric  subgroups  of  the essential
  subgroups.  These  are  found  from  the  subgroup  lattices  of  the groups
  [22XE_i/Z(E_i)[122X,  their  [22XF[122X-classes and automizers from the lazy graph. A class is
  only  expanded  as  far as it is needed: the test abandons the [22XF[122X-class of an
  essential  subgroup, or of an [22XS[122X-centric subgroup of one, at the first member
  which  is  not  [22XS[122X-centric  or has a larger normalizer in [22XS[122X, as such a member
  already  decides the condition in question. (For a candidate system which is
  far  from  saturated  such  a  class  may  be  enormous, for instance when a
  subgroup of an abelian essential subgroup with a large automizer lies in it,
  while its first bad member is found at once.) This is how the package avoids
  the fusion graph of [PS21], whose construction (the subgroup lattice of [22XS[122X up
  to  [22XB[122X-conjugacy  and  the  [22XAut_F(E)[122X-orbits of all subgroups of all essential
  subgroups) is the most expensive part of the original implementation.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0Y[13XClassification.[113X [2XAllFusionSystems[102X ([14X3.2-1[114X) follows [PS21, Section 4]: for each
  [22Xp'[122X-subgroup  [22XK[122X  of  [22XAut(S)[122X  up  to  conjugacy  ([22XAut_F(S)  =  Inn(S) ⋊ K[122X) the
  proto-essential  subgroups  are  grouped  into [22XB[122X-classes; the candidates for
  [22XAut_F(P)[122X are the subgroups [22XA[122X of [22XAut(P)[122X with [22XAut_S(P)[122X as Sylow [22Xp[122X-subgroup and
  [22XA/Inn(P)[122X  having  a  strongly [22Xp[122X-embedded subgroup, one for each [22XAut(P)[122X-class
  (all conjugates of such a subgroup containing [22XAut_S(P)[122X as a Sylow [22Xp[122X-subgroup
  are  conjugate  under  [22XN_Aut(P)(Aut_S(P))[122X), expanded to the conjugates whose
  Sylow  normalizer  contains [22XAut_N_B(P)(P)[122X, with equality when [22XN_S(P)[122X lies in
  no   [22XB[122X-conjugate   of   a   proto-essential   subgroup;   the   subsets   of
  proto-essentials and the assignments of automizers are run through up to the
  action of [22XN_Aut(S)(Aut_F(S))[122X; the resulting systems are tested for [22XO^p(F)=F[122X,
  [22XO_p(F)=1[122X and saturation and finally reduced up to isomorphism. The automizer
  candidates are enumerated by the cyclic extension method with the hereditary
  bound  [22X|H|_p  ≤  |Out_S(P)|[122X;  this  is  complete whenever GAP can handle the
  subgroup  lattice  of  [22XOut(P)[122X. For elementary abelian [22XP[122X with a huge [22XOut(P) =
  GL(n,p)[122X   the   package   falls   back   to   the  monomial  groups  of  the
  [22XAut_S(P)[122X-invariant   frames  (and  reports  this  through  [2XInfoFusionSystems[102X
  ([14X3.2-2[114X)),  or  uses  candidates  supplied  by  the  user  through the option
  [10Xcandidates[110X.[133X
